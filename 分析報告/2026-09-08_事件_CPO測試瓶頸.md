- **交叉佐證(獨立來源)**:[TrendForce](https://insights.trendforce.com/p/cpo-testing-market-opportunities)「CPO 量產關鍵瓶頸是測試」;[SemiEngineering](https://semiengineering.com/co-packaged-optics-testing-faces-steep-data-center-ramp/);Digitimes 2026-09-04(致茂 Chroma:測試前移中段以避後段昂貴失效);單顆 CPO PIC 全光學檢測平均 >100 秒。【事實,中高】
